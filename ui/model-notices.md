## Local transcription engines and model weights

These are not bundled in the web UI or the anyscribe package; local setup
installs the engines and downloads the weights on your computer. Credited here
because anyscribe uses them.

- **Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3** by NVIDIA, CC BY 4.0, converted to ONNX by istupakov

  https://huggingface.co/istupakov/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-onnx).
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2** by NVIDIA, CC BY 4.0, converted to ONNX by istupakov
  (https://huggingface.co/istupakov/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2-onnx).
- **onnx-asr** by Ilya Stupakov, MIT License (https://github.com/istupakov/onnx-asr).
- **Silero VAD** by Silero Team, MIT License (https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad),
  ONNX copy from https://huggingface.co/istupakov/silero-vad-onnx.
- **faster-whisper** by SYSTRAN, MIT License; Whisper weights by OpenAI, MIT License.
